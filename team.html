Working structure

Co-founder Framework

How Hasna and Milan divide decisions, work together, and keep the personal relationship protected while building a company together.

Tier 1 — Hasna decides

Commercial and domain decisions within the CEO mandate

Client pricing Sales strategy Brand direction Compliance positioning Partnership terms Investor narrative

Tier 2 — Milan decides

Technical and product decisions within the CTO mandate

Tech stack Infrastructure Build vs buy AI model selection Release schedule Security

Tier 3 — Both must agree

Decisions that define the company or the relationship

Equity structure Fundraising terms Major pivots Senior hires Exit decisions

Weekly cadence

Monday

Co-founder sync (30 min)

What are we each working on? Any blockers?

Thursday

Product check-in (30 min)

Build progress. Joint decisions needed?

Monthly

Metrics & direction (1 hr)

KPIs, pipeline, runway, what's changing.

Annually

Co-founder reset

Full review: roles, equity, what would change.

Boundaries

Work has a container

Amaea conversations stay in working hours. Not at dinner, not in bed. The container makes the rest of life safe.

The co-founder hat is removable

Milan is the CTO at work. He is the partner at home. If it's unclear which conversation this is — ask.

Monthly founder date

One evening a month for the future — not operational, not problem-solving. Why are we doing this?
