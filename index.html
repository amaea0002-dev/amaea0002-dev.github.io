Amaea is the compliance intelligence platform for FCA-regulated firms. This document defines who we are, what we believe, and how we work.

The foundation

What we stand for

Everything we build, every decision we make, and every relationship we form is rooted in these statements.

Mission

To remove chaos from compliance.

Our reason for existing. If a decision doesn't help remove chaos from compliance, it's not our decision to make.

Vision

To be the platform that powers compliance for FCA regulated industries.

Starting with UK independent financial advisers. Expanding to every FCA-regulated sector that follows.

Brand tagline

Stay compliant. Stay confident.

What we believe

Values

These are the principles that shape how we work, how we build, and how we treat each other — every day, not just when it's easy.

◻

Simplicity

We make complex things clear — in our product and in how we work. Clarity is not a nice-to-have. It is our competitive advantage.

◆

Integrity

Trust is the foundation of compliance and of our relationships. We do what we say, and we say what we mean.

◎

Build for People

We build technology that genuinely helps people do their jobs better. Real problems, real solutions — not features for a pitch deck.

△

Respect

We treat each other with professionalism, fairness, and trust — especially when we disagree. Respect is not optional.

↗

Learn and Improve

We continuously develop ourselves, our product, and our thinking. Standing still in compliance is falling behind.

How we work

Operating Principles

These are the standards we hold ourselves to. Not aspirations — behaviours.

01

Work Flexibly, Deliver Consistently

We trust people to manage their time and work in ways that suit them. What matters is delivering high-quality work and meeting commitments. Flexibility is earned through consistency, not assumed.

02

Decisions Backed by Evidence

Important decisions should be supported by data, reasoning, and evidence — not hierarchy or assumptions. We question before we accept. We challenge before we commit.

03

Sustainable Performance

Great work should not require burnout. We aim for sustainable pace and healthy workloads. We are building for the long game — and that requires people who are well, not worn out.

04

Share Knowledge

We work as a team. Information should be accessible and shared so everyone can learn, contribute, and make better decisions. Knowledge hoarding slows us all down.

05

Develop People

We invest in growth through feedback, learning, and clear career development. We want the people who work here to be better because they were here.

06

Solve Real Problems

Our focus is always on solving meaningful problems for the people who rely on our product. We build what matters — not what is easy to demo.

The lines

Non-Negotiables

These are not guidelines. They are the conditions under which Amaea operates. All of them. Always.

No.

Politics

Decisions are made based on evidence and what is best for the company and our users — not hierarchy, ego, or internal politics. We leave that at the door.

No.

Disrespect

Professionalism and fairness are expected at all times. We treat each other with respect — especially when we disagree. This is non-negotiable.

No.

Avoiding Responsibility

If something goes wrong, we address it and fix it. Blame and defensiveness are not productive. Ownership is. We do not look for someone else to point at.

No.

Burnout Culture

Sustainable performance matters. Overtime should not be the default way work gets done. We protect each other's capacity to perform over time, not just this week.

No.

Silos

Knowledge should be shared. Collaboration is how we build better systems. Working in isolation helps no one and weakens the whole.
