Product Roadmap

From foundation to public launch · September 2028. Each item carries an owner and a delivery confidence.

Why these phases, in this order

Phase 1 · Foundation. Build the things every IFA needs day one: dashboard, document import, flag system, FCA knowledge base, AI assistant. Without these the product isn't real.

Phase 2 · Intelligence. The compounding advantage — annual review auto-generation, deeper Consumer Duty support, vulnerable-client monitoring, second integration. This is where switching cost starts to build.

Phase 3 · Scale. Features that matter at multi-adviser firms — board-level reporting, FCA visit toolkit, custom reports, full integration suite. Sets up the move beyond the 5–15 adviser segment.

Phase 4 · Launch. Public launch September 2028 with the enterprise tier, custom AI training, and a Customer Success offering. The first three phases earn the right to charge a premium tier.
