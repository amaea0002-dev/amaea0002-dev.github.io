CTO Working Agreement

This Working Agreement sets out operational expectations for Milan Sajiv's role as Chief Technology Officer of Amaea (the "Business"), the pre-incorporation venture intended to be incorporated as Amaea Ltd ("the Company").

It is subordinate to, and consistent with, the Amaea Co-Founder & Shareholder Agreement dated [EFFECTIVE DATE] (the "Founder Agreement"). Where this Working Agreement is silent or unclear, the Founder Agreement governs.

PURPOSE. This document captures the day-to-day operational expectations of the CTO role. It is not an employment contract — Milan is a Co-Founder, not an employee, and is not subject to statutory employment rights through this document. Salary, equity, IP, leaver provisions, and non-compete are all governed by the Founder Agreement.

1. Role & Domain Authority

1.1 Role title

Chief Technology Officer & Co-Founder.

1.2 Domain authority

Per Clause 4.2 of the Founder Agreement, Milan has decision authority within his domain. Specifically:

  • Technical architecture — system design choices, framework selection, infrastructure decisions
  • AI systems — model selection, prompt design, retrieval architecture, AI safety and rate-limiting
  • Security — authentication, authorisation, data protection, infrastructure security posture
  • Engineering hiring — sourcing, interviewing, and hiring engineers within agreed headcount budget (subject to Reserved Matter Schedule 1.9 for senior-engineer hires above £75k)
  • Code quality — coding standards, review processes, technical debt management
  • Technical roadmap — ordering of engineering work, technical feasibility veto on commercial proposals
  • Data architecture — schema design, data pipelines, integration architecture, FCA knowledge base
  • Technical vendor selection — Supabase, Vercel, Anthropic, Voyage AI, Resend, etc.

1.3 Peer governance, not subordinate

Milan does not report to the CEO. Milan reports jointly with the CEO to the Board of the Company (which is composed of the two Founders pre-investor). Domain conflicts are resolved per Clause 4 and Clause 11.3 of the Founder Agreement.

2. Time Commitment

2.1 Pre-Incorporation phase (today → Incorporation Date, targeted Q3 2027)

Approximately 10 hours per week, flexed to demand. This recognises that Milan retains primary outside employment.

2.2 Pre-launch phase (Incorporation → Public Launch, Q3 2028)

Reviewed at Incorporation in joint discussion with CEO. Expected to remain in the 10–15 hours/week range unless commercial circumstances change.

2.3 Post-launch phase (Public Launch onwards)

The financial model contemplates Milan going full-time when the Business reaches ~150 active customers (per the v2 step function in amaea-financial-model-scenarios.md). The transition to full-time is at Milan's discretion in good-faith consultation with the CEO; not unilaterally imposed by the CEO.

2.4 Quality over hours

Time tracking is not a performance metric. The expectation is consistent measurable progress against agreed technical deliverables, not adherence to a rigid hours total. The 10 hours/week figure is the planning baseline, not a contractual minimum below which Milan is in breach.

2.5 Outside work

Milan is expressly permitted to retain his existing employment, including current paid role outside the Business. He may not, however, take any other engagement that materially competes with the Business or that meaningfully diminishes his ability to perform CTO duties — see Clause 5.2 of the Founder Agreement.

3. Responsibilities

3.1 Core deliverables

Milan is responsible for:

  • Designing and maintaining the system architecture of the Amaea platform
  • Assessing technical feasibility of proposed product features
  • Identifying and integrating AI capabilities (Claude, Voyage, etc.)
  • Defining data pipelines and third-party integrations
  • Owning the live MVP (shipped and hardened May 2026) and shipping Phase 2 + Phase 3 features against the roadmap
  • Maintaining production systems (app.amaea.co.uk) including uptime, security, and cost
  • Recruiting and managing engineering hires when budget permits
  • Maintaining security and data-protection posture
  • Producing technical documentation to a quality that allows the Business to onboard new technical hires without bottlenecking on Milan
  • Maintaining a working financial model from a technical-feasibility standpoint (cost estimates for cloud, AI, integrations)

3.2 Joint deliverables (with CEO)

  • Product roadmap (technical-feasibility + commercial-priority blend)
  • Hiring plan
  • Annual budget
  • Founder calls + customer demos (alternating lead)

4. Working Structure

4.1 Joint sessions

At minimum one joint working session per week with the CEO. Cadence and format (call, in-person, async update) by mutual agreement.

4.2 Async communication

For day-to-day, async tools are the default (Slack, email, shared docs). Synchronous time is reserved for genuinely collaborative work and decisions.

4.3 Cadence reviews

The Founders shall review this Working Agreement (cadence, commitment, responsibilities) at least:

  • At the Incorporation Date
  • At each transition between phases (pre-launch → post-launch → multi-sector expansion)
  • On request from either Founder, with at least 14 days' notice

5. Compensation

5.1 Pre-Incorporation

No cash compensation. Milan retains his outside employment income and contributes labour as a Founder. The equity granted under the Founder Agreement is the principal consideration for this period.

5.2 Post-Incorporation

Until the Business generates sufficient cash flow to support founder compensation:

  • No salary from the Business; Milan's outside salary continues to cover personal living costs.
  • No dividends to Milan, except (a) pari passu with Hasna if dividends are ever declared to all shareholders, or (b) by separate written agreement.

5.3 Founder-comp trigger

When the Business reaches the milestones modelled in amaea-financial-model-scenarios.md (v2) — specifically, ≥150 active customers (the "Milan full-time" step function) — Milan may transition to full-time, with a market-rate salary agreed between the Founders as a Reserved Matter (Schedule 1.11).

5.4 No tax-inefficient compensation

No salary is paid below the income tax / NI thresholds where doing so would create administrative burden disproportionate to the benefit. Milan and the Company shall use SEIS-eligible share consideration as the primary form of value transfer pre-revenue.

5.5 Expense reimbursement

Reasonable business expenses (cloud bills paid personally, software subscriptions used for Business, travel to customer/partner meetings) are reimbursable by the Company on production of receipts. Pre-Incorporation expenses are tracked and reimbursed at Incorporation.

6. Communication & Disagreements

6.1 Communication principles

Milan commits to:

  • Communicating technical constraints clearly and early
  • Flagging blockers before they become critical
  • Challenging product / strategy ideas constructively, in private not in front of investors or third parties
  • Aligning with the CEO weekly on priorities, progress, and risks

6.2 Resolving disagreements

Per Clause 11.3 of the Founder Agreement. In short: discuss, mediate, expert-determination, shotgun. Most disagreements should be resolved at the first stage.

6.3 Confidentiality of disputes

Until escalated under the Founder Agreement dispute mechanism, any disagreement is treated as confidential between the Founders. Neither Founder shall raise the disagreement publicly, with employees, investors, customers, or third parties without the other Founder's consent.

7. Values, Mission & Vision

Milan commits to:

  • Upholding Amaea's values in all work and decisions
  • Contributing to and supporting Amaea's mission and vision
  • Building with focus on long-term impact, scalability, quality, and regulatory rigour
  • Prioritising the Business's reputation in the UK IFA market over short-term shortcuts

8. Commitment to Quality

Milan will prioritise:

  • Scalable and realistic architecture
  • Simplicity in technical design — preferring small, well-understood systems over premature complexity
  • Building only what is necessary for the current MVP / phase
  • Test coverage on all critical paths
  • Audit trail and data integrity for compliance-grade reliability (this is a compliance platform; the bar is higher than typical SaaS)

9. Exit & Transition

If Milan wishes to step away from the CTO role:

  • Notice: Eight (8) weeks' written notice per Clause 9.3 of the Founder Agreement.
  • Handover: Complete written technical handover to a successor or to the CEO if no successor is in place.
  • Hiring assist: Reasonable assistance in sourcing and onboarding a replacement, including interviews and technical assessment of candidates.
  • Code custody: All Business code, credentials, and IP transferred fully to the Company within the notice period.
  • Equity: Vesting and leaver provisions per Clauses 3 and 9 of the Founder Agreement.

10. Future Evolution

This Working Agreement may evolve as Amaea progresses. Both Founders agree to revisit it at the cadence in Clause 4.3, and to update it (in writing, signed by both) to reflect material changes in Milan's role, time commitment, or compensation.

11. Relationship to Founder Agreement

Where this Working Agreement is silent, the Founder Agreement governs.

Where this Working Agreement conflicts with the Founder Agreement, the Founder Agreement prevails.

Where the Founder Agreement is silent, this Working Agreement governs operational expectations.
