Sector Research

Each is a ~10,000-word working document covering regulation, market sizing, segmentation, named target firms, compliance pain points, competitive landscape, pricing benchmarks, and go-to-market notes. Reviewed quarterly; staggered so one comes due each quarter. Last reviewed 18 May 2026.

Each card below summarises the memo. Click Read full memo → to read the rendered ~10,000-word document on the intranet. Cadence · How this compounds

Why we keep these living, and what to do at each review

Each memo carries a "Next review" date staggered across the year so one comes due each quarter. The aim is that when Amaea launches in any sector, the team is 4 years deep on the market, not 0 days. Cold-starting a new sector costs 3–6 months; compounding research now is near-free. At each quarterly review: verify regulatory citations are still current, update market size figures, resolve the flagged gaps, add new competitive entrants, append a row to that memo's update log table.
Cross-cutting findings from the May 2026 initial pass: sector ARPA varies wildly (Wealth £5–30k/mo vs Accountants £99–599/mo) — the v3 financial model needs sector-specific ARPA tables, not a uniform 70%-of-IFA discount. 110+ named target firms across all four sectors — usable directly for 2030+ outreach.
