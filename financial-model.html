Financial planning

Financial Model

Three-scenario bootstrap-fundable model. Bear / Base / Bull projections across 60 months (May 2026 → Apr 2031) backed by three Google Sheets workbooks. Current version: v2

Overview

Three scenarios

All three scenarios share identical pricing (£2,094 blended ARPA), retention (10% annual churn), cost structure, and the £8,000 founder capital contribution at incorporation. They diverge only on Founders fill rate and post-launch funnel rate, driven by how many distribution channels land.

Bear

~110 firms

90 Founders + 20 funnel by Dec 2028. No major distribution channels land. Pure founder-led outbound.

Base — plan around this

~180 firms

150 Founders + 30 funnel. Intelliflo partnership confirmed; CISI endorsement warming.

Bull

~280 firms

200 Founders maxed + 80 funnel. All four channels: Intelliflo marketplace, CISI, Robin Powell, FP Advance.

Numbers above are at 90 days post-launch (Dec 2028). Y3 (Apr 2029) projections in the table below.

Cost scaling · v2

Formulas in the Monthly tab

v2 cost rows use time-varying ramps + per-customer scaling. People includes engineer step functions. S&M adds a revenue floor. Office toggles on at >100 customers. Recruitment fees auto-detect hire onboardings. References active customers via Row 12 (total active = Founders + full-price). Paste into the B-column cell, drag right to BI.

People — cell B22

=IF(B$2<29,0,IF(B$2<32,5041,9041)) + MAX(0,B12-50)*50 + IF(B12>30,8000,0) + IF(B12>100,8000,0) + IF(B12>150,10000,0)
  • Months 1–28: £0 (Hasna at Lucent, no Amaea payroll)
  • Months 29–31: £5,041 (Hasna £1,041 + Sales hire £4,000)
  • Month 32+: £9,041 (CX manager onboards)
  • +£50 per active customer above 50 — CS / support headcount scaling
  • +£8,000 when active > 30 / > 100 / > 150: first engineer / second engineer / Milan full-time

S&M — cell B23 v2 fix: revenue floor

=IF(B$2<17,0,IF(B$2<29,600+MAX(0,B12-50)*20,MAX(2700+MAX(0,B12-50)*20,B17*0.08)))
  • Months 1–16: £0 (pre-incorporation)
  • Months 17–28: £600 + £20/customer scaling
  • Month 29+: MAX of: base (£2,700 + £20/customer scaling) or 8% of MRR (revenue floor)
  • Revenue floor takes over at high MRR — S&M scales realistically with the company, not stuck at the base level

G&A — cell B24

=IF(B$2<17,0,IF(B$2<29,675+MAX(0,B12-50)*5,1225+MAX(0,B12-50)*5))
  • Months 1–16: £0
  • Months 17–28: £675 + £5/customer above 50 (accountancy + legal + Claude Max + software)
  • Month 29+: £1,225 + £5/customer (adds insurance + more software seats)

Office — cell B25 v2 new

=IF(B12>100,2000,0)
  • £2,000/mo small London office or co-working when active customers exceed 100
  • Triggers in Y4 for Bear, late Y3 for Base, mid-Y3 for Bull

Recruitment fees — cell B26 v2 new

=IF(B22-(prev B22)>=3500,12000,0)
  • Detects month-over-month People-cost jumps ≥£3,500 (a new hire onboarded)
  • Adds £12,000 one-off — ≈15% of average loaded salary at UK tech recruiter fees
  • Fires automatically when Sales / CX / engineers / Milan-FT come on board

Evolution

Version history

v1.0

Initial build · 16 May 2026

8-section assumptions doc filled. 3 Google Sheets workbooks built from CSV starter pack. Flat cost rows. Hasna salary starting Q1 2028. Scenarios named Base / Probable / Best.

v1.1

Hasna salary timing fix

Hasna stays at Lucent until Sept 2028 (public launch). Updated People formula to start at month 29, not month 21. Saves ~£8k in incorrectly modelled pre-revenue burn.

v1.2

Customer-driven cost scaling

Added linear scaling to People (£50/customer), S&M (£20/customer), G&A (£5/customer) above the 50-customer threshold. Recognises that CS, content, and software seats scale with book size. Net margins drop from 93–96% to 85–88%. Scenarios renamed Bear / Base / Bull for investor consistency.

v1.3

Engineer step functions

Three engineer hire triggers in the People row: first engineer at >30 customers, second at >100, Milan full-time at >150. Net margins drop to 79–83% — at the upper bound of real SaaS norms (60–75%) and defensible for investor due diligence.

v2

60-month horizon · Founders discount modelled · realistic cost scaling — current

Extended to 60 months (May 2026 → Apr 2031) so Y3 + new Y5 are both captured. Founders 20% lifetime discount properly modelled via cohort-split MRR (no mental adjustment). S&M with 8% revenue floor — scales realistically at high MRR. Recruitment fees: auto-detected £12k spike per hire onboarding. Office: toggles on at >100 active customers (+£2k/mo). COGS sensitivity row (£50 baseline vs £75 high-usage). Steady-state acquisition rates calibrated per-scenario (3 / 8 / 14 per month) instead of all collapsing to 6/mo.

Caveats · v2

Known limitations (v3 candidates)

v2 closed the four biggest v1.3 limitations (Founders discount, 60-month horizon, S&M floor, recruitment fees, office). These are the remaining gaps — carry forward to v3 if/when the model needs to support sector or international expansion conversations.

  1. Sector expansion not modelled. v2 assumes UK IFA only forever. Real plan is to expand into mortgage brokers / insurance intermediaries / wealth managers / accountants. v3 should add a "sector mix" dimension. See sector expansion timing below for the trigger logic.
  2. NRR expansion not modelled (tier upgrades, seat add-ons, premium reports). Real SaaS NRR > 100% is common.
  3. International expansion not modelled. v3 should add country-by-country acquisition + cost lines.
  4. Anthropic API spend assumption (£50/customer/mo) unverified. No production data yet. Revisit when 5+ Founders live. v2 already includes a B34 sensitivity row at £75/customer.
  5. Customer concentration not modelled. At Y5 Bull (593 customers), single firms representing >10% of ARR would be a real risk to track separately.
  6. Hire timing detected via cost jumps (heuristic). Works in practice but v3 could add explicit "hire month" input cells.
Process

How to update the model

Adjusting a scenario (e.g. tweaking Base ramp)

  • Open the Google Sheets workbook for Bear / Base / Bull
  • Edit cells in Monthly tab Row 5 (New customers)
  • All downstream cells (Active customers Row 7, MRR Row 10, ARR Row 11) recompute automatically
  • Annual tab and UnitEcon tab reflect the change

Adjusting cost assumptions

  • Edit formulas in B15 (People), B16 (S&M), B17 (G&A); drag right to AK
  • Per-customer scaling factors: edit the multiplier (50, 20, 5)
  • Step function thresholds: edit the IF conditions
