Financial planning

Financial Model

Three-scenario bootstrap-fundable model built 16 May 2026. Bear / Base / Bull projections across 36 months (May 2026 → Apr 2029) backed by three Google Sheets workbooks. Current version: v1.3

Overview

Three scenarios

All three scenarios share identical pricing (£2,094 blended ARPA), retention (10% annual churn), cost structure, and the £8,000 founder capital contribution at incorporation. They diverge only on Founders fill rate and post-launch funnel rate, driven by how many distribution channels land.

Bear

~110 firms

90 Founders + 20 funnel by Dec 2028. No major distribution channels land. Pure founder-led outbound.

Base — plan around this

~180 firms

150 Founders + 30 funnel. Intelliflo partnership confirmed; CISI endorsement warming.

Bull

~280 firms

200 Founders maxed + 80 funnel. All four channels: Intelliflo marketplace, CISI, Robin Powell, FP Advance.

Numbers above are at 90 days post-launch (Dec 2028). Y3 (Apr 2029) projections in the table below.

Cost scaling — v1.3

Formulas in the Monthly tab

Three rows in the Monthly tab use time-varying ramps plus per-customer scaling. People also includes engineer step functions. Paste into the B-column cell, drag right to AK.

People — cell B15

=IF(B$2<29,0,IF(B$2<32,5041,9041)) + MAX(0, B7 - 50) * 50 + IF(B7 > 30, 8000, 0) + IF(B7 > 100, 8000, 0) + IF(B7 > 150, 10000, 0)
  • Months 1–28: £0 (Hasna at Lucent, no Amaea payroll)
  • Months 29–31: £5,041 (Hasna £1,041 + Sales hire £4,000 onboard at public launch)
  • Month 32+: £9,041 (CX manager onboards)
  • +£50 per active customer above 50 — CS / support headcount scaling
  • +£8,000 when active > 30: first engineer hire (~£90k loaded London mid-level)
  • +£8,000 when active > 100: second engineer
  • +£10,000 when active > 150: Milan goes full-time (~£120k loaded)

S&M — cell B16

=IF(B$2<17,0,IF(B$2<29,600,2700)) + MAX(0, B7 - 50) * 20
  • Months 1–16: £0 (pre-incorporation)
  • Months 17–28: £600 (content + outbound tooling)
  • Month 29+: £2,700 (content + paid acquisition + events + tooling)
  • +£20 per active customer above 50 — content / events / paid scaling with book size

G&A — cell B17

=IF(B$2<17,0,IF(B$2<29,675,1225)) + MAX(0, B7 - 50) * 5
  • Months 1–16: £0
  • Months 17–28: £675 (accountancy + legal + Claude Max + software)
  • Month 29+: £1,225 (adds insurance + more software seats)
  • +£5 per active customer above 50 — modest team-size proxy

Evolution

Version history

v1.0

Initial build · 16 May 2026

8-section assumptions doc filled. 3 Google Sheets workbooks built from CSV starter pack. Flat cost rows. Hasna salary starting Q1 2028. Scenarios named Base / Probable / Best.

v1.1

Hasna salary timing fix

Hasna stays at Lucent until Sept 2028 (public launch). Updated People formula to start at month 29, not month 21. Saves ~£8k in incorrectly modelled pre-revenue burn.

v1.2

Customer-driven cost scaling

Added linear scaling to People (£50/customer), S&M (£20/customer), G&A (£5/customer) above the 50-customer threshold. Recognises that CS, content, and software seats scale with book size. Net margins drop from 93–96% to 85–88%. Scenarios renamed Bear / Base / Bull for investor consistency.

v1.3

Engineer step functions — current

Three engineer hire triggers in the People row: first engineer at >30 customers, second at >100, Milan full-time at >150. Net margins drop to 79–83% — at the upper bound of real SaaS norms (60–75%) and defensible for investor due diligence.

Caveats

Known limitations

These are real gaps in the v1.3 model — flag in investor conversations if asked. Carry forward to v2 if/when the model needs to support a fundraise.

  1. Founders 20% lifetime discount not modelled in CSV. Spreadsheet applies blended ARPA (£2,094) to all customers — overstates ARR by ~15%. Numbers above adjust for this manually. v2 fix: add a "% Founders in cohort" row.
  2. S&M doesn't scale steeply enough at high revenue. At £6M ARR a real company would spend £500k–1.5M/yr on marketing. Bull case shows £90k S&M which is light.
  3. No recruitment fees modelled. Each hire typically costs 15–20% of base salary in agency fees — a one-off £10–15k spike per hire.
  4. Office / co-working £0. Assumes work-from-home indefinitely. Realistic at Bear, less so at Bull.
  5. Anthropic API spend assumption (£8/customer/mo) unverified. No production data yet (1 test firm as of May 2026). Revisit when 5+ Founders live.
  6. 36-month horizon ends April 2029 — only 8 months post-launch. The "Year 3 ARR" target of £2.6M from the assumptions doc is end of 2031, beyond this model. v2 should extend to 60 months.
Process

How to update the model

Adjusting a scenario (e.g. tweaking Base ramp)

  • Open the Google Sheets workbook for Bear / Base / Bull
  • Edit cells in Monthly tab Row 5 (New customers)
  • All downstream cells (Active customers Row 7, MRR Row 10, ARR Row 11) recompute automatically
  • Annual tab and UnitEcon tab reflect the change

Adjusting cost assumptions

  • Edit formulas in B15 (People), B16 (S&M), B17 (G&A); drag right to AK
  • Per-customer scaling factors: edit the multiplier (50, 20, 5)
  • Step function thresholds: edit the IF conditions
