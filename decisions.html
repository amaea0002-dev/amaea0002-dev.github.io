Decision Log

Key product, commercial and operational decisions with rationale. Keeps us from relitigating past choices.

15 May 2026 Confirmed
19-type document taxonomy with Zod-validated schemas
Classify every uploaded compliance document into one of 19 canonical IFA types, then extract per-type fields against a Zod schema. Lenient enum coercion handles case/spacing variance from Claude's vision output.
Why: Loose JSON extraction returned inconsistent shapes that broke downstream code. Per-type Zod schemas force the extraction to either succeed cleanly or surface a typed error — never silent corruption. The taxonomy was chosen by mapping real Lucent documents to FCA rulebook obligations, then collapsing synonyms (e.g. SOA / ROA / Suitability Letter → one type). 19 types covers the entire IFA compliance file based on the May 2026 corpus review.
7 May 2026 Confirmed
Bootstrap to public launch; no pre-launch seed
Year 1 total cost ~£20–35k, funded by personal savings. Milan takes no Amaea salary while working full-time elsewhere. Hasna takes a tax-efficient £12k salary + £50k dividend (~£62k gross) once Lucent employment ends.
Why: With ~70–80% gross margins and a strong GTM stack (Robin Powell + CISI + Intelliflo + Founders Programme), Amaea should be self-funding from early scale. ~2 Professional firms = break-even, ~5 Professional firms = full dividend coverage. Raising VC pre-launch would dilute ownership for a problem we don't have. Raise from strength later, if at all.
April 2026 Confirmed
Compliance health score formula: 40 / 35 / 25
Live 0–100 firm health score = 40% document completeness + 35% review completion + 25% inverse flag severity (critical=15, high=8, medium=3, low=1 weighted penalty).
Why: An IFA's compliance posture is dominated by documentation gaps (which break FCA evidencing) and overdue annual reviews (which break COBS 9.5). Flag severity matters but must be weighted lower because it's already partly captured by the doc/review components. The weights produce intuitive results on the Sterling demo firm (88/100) and the formula is documented in the platform docs so we can defend it under FCA review.
May 2026 Confirmed
IFA-first go-to-market, not networks
Target boutique IFA firms directly rather than going through networks or DA groups.
Why: Networks have long procurement cycles and impose constraints on pricing and product scope. Direct IFA relationships give us faster feedback loops, more pricing control, and real usage data before we scale.
May 2026 Confirmed
Read-only integrations at MVP
Intelliflo integration is read-only at launch; we pull data in, we never write back to the source system.
Why: Write access creates liability risk (we could corrupt a CRM) and requires a much deeper security review process. Read-only is sufficient to deliver core value at MVP and significantly reduces our risk surface.
April 2026 Confirmed
SaaS pricing by firm size, not per client
Price by adviser count, not per managed client record.
Why: Per-client pricing penalises growth and creates unpredictable costs for the firm. IFAs want flat, predictable SaaS fees. Adviser-count tiers align our revenue growth with firm growth without creating friction.
April 2026 Confirmed
UK-only launch, no international before 2030
Launch exclusively in the UK market. No international expansion until at least 2030.
Why: FCA Consumer Duty is a UK-specific regulatory driver. International markets have entirely different compliance frameworks that would require separate product work. Focus beats optionality at this stage.
March 2026 Confirmed
Founders programme as GTM entry point
Use SP Advanced, Robin Powell, CISI, and Intelliflo as early access channels. 200 founder spots with 20% lifetime discount.
Why: These channels concentrate compliance-aware IFAs who would be natural early adopters. The 200-spot scarcity creates urgency. The lifetime discount rewards early commitment and builds long-term loyalty before we have social proof.
15 May 2026 Confirmed
Demo form delivery: switched from formsubmit.co to a Vercel serverless function using Resend
New Vercel serverless function api/demo.js on the amaea.ai project calls the Resend HTTP API directly. Routes to milan@ and hasna@ via a comma-separated DEMO_TO_EMAIL env var. The old amaea0002@gmail.com address was purged from the codebase.
Why: formsubmit.co verification for the new amaea.co.uk email was stuck behind a provider outage. Resend was already verified for the domain (SPF/DKIM), so a single-file serverless function was faster to ship and removes a third-party dependency we no longer need.

15 May 2026 Confirmed
Live AI chat on the marketing site dropped; reverted to auto-loop animation only
Briefly integrated a live /api/public/chat input on the homepage. Reverted: the auto-typing demo animation conflicts with live request/response timing, creating overlapping outputs. The looping animation is retained as a passive showcase.
Why: The demo is more reliable — and more legible — as a passive "show, don't interact" feature at this stage. A real try-it-yourself interface belongs in the product, not the marketing site, and requires session isolation we haven't built.
15 May 2026 Confirmed
RMAR Section F pre-population shipped without a DB migration
The Section F aggregator queries the existing documents table directly using type='complaint_record' and status='complete' with a period filter. No new table or schema change was required for Slice 1.
Why: Avoids touching the CHECK constraint on documents and ships Slice 1 today. A migration approach can be revisited if Slices 2 or 3 require it. Inline DISP 1.6 8-week breach flags surface on the Section F page at the moment compliance officers are reviewing complaints — persistent cron-raised flags deferred to a later slice.
