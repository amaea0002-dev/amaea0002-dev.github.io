The pitch in one paragraph

What Amaea is, why now, why us

Amaea is the FCA compliance platform UK Independent Financial Adviser firms have needed since Consumer Duty came into force in 2023. We replace the spreadsheets, fragmented file checks, and reactive supervision that every IFA practice uses today with a single live dashboard that reads the documents IFAs already produce, extracts the compliance-critical fields with AI vision, and surfaces every gap with a specific FCA rule citation. The product is built; the FCA knowledge base (11,645 chunks) is indexed; the document extraction pipeline handles 21 IFA compliance document types (19 client-level + 2 firm-level) with auto-flag-raising; the AI assistant grounds every answer in retrieval, never hallucination.

Why now: Consumer Duty (PS22/9) made compliance evidencing a board-level concern. Claude Sonnet 4.6's vision performance on real IFA documents finally clears the bar a junior compliance officer sets. The FCA has been data-led in supervision since 2022 — firms that look bad on data are identifiable before a visit. Why us: Hasna is a financial-planning compliance specialist at a UK IFA; she speaks at FP Advance, is interviewed by Robin Powell, and is known to the CISI CEO. Milan is a software engineer building the technical product end-to-end. The team has the regulatory authority to sell and the engineering depth to build.

~5,000 IFA firms in the UK · average revenue per firm assumed at ~£1,750/month across our tiers · addressable revenue ceiling of ~£90M ARR. This is the entire MVP target market — we don't need to leave it to build a very large business.

Initial focus: 5–15 adviser firms — large enough to feel the Consumer Duty burden, small enough not to have enterprise compliance tooling already in place.

Funding philosophy

Default: bootstrap. The v2 financial model is cash-flow-positive from month 2 of revenue across Bear, Base, and Bull scenarios. £8k founder contribution at incorporation is the only outside capital needed. Founders revenue funds all hires and growth from Q4 2027 onwards. Y5 (Apr 2031) cash ranges from £7.87M (Bear) to £23.51M (Bull) — entirely bootstrapped.

A raise enters scope only when both of these are true:

  • Multi-sector expansion is active — Amaea has live customers beyond UK IFA (mortgage brokers, insurance intermediaries, wealth managers, accountants).
  • International expansion is active — Amaea has live customers in at least one market outside the UK.

Both are post-2030 milestones. Until then, no raise. Materials tracked below are maintained for that optionality, and for adjacent uses (advisor conversations, partnership discussions, customer demos).
