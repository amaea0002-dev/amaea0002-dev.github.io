Co-Founder & Shareholder Agreement

Amaea Ltd (in formation — incorporation targeted Q3 2027). Governs the rights and obligations of the Co-Founders to each other and to the Business.

This Co-Founder & Shareholder Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between:

  • Milan Sajiv ("Milan"), an individual residing in the United Kingdom; and
  • Hasna Sahul Hameed ("Hasna"), an individual residing in the United Kingdom

(each a "Founder", together the "Founders") in respect of the business commonly known as Amaea (the "Business"), which the Founders intend to incorporate as a UK private limited company ("Amaea Ltd" or the "Company").

This Agreement governs the Founders' rights and obligations to each other and to the Business, both before the Company is incorporated and after incorporation. On incorporation, the Founders intend for the Company to adopt this Agreement (or its substantive terms in a form negotiated with legal counsel and the Company's then-shareholders).

DRAFT STATUS. This is a working draft prepared 2026-05-16 to replace an earlier "Milan's Contract" working agreement. Both Founders should review independently, then jointly bring this to a UK startup lawyer (e.g. SeedLegals, Linkilaw, or equivalent boutique firm) for validation before signing. Estimated lawyer cost for review + refinement: £500–£1,500.

2. Equity Split & Share Issuance

2.1 Agreed equity split

On the Incorporation Date, ordinary shares in the Company shall be issued as follows:

  • Milan: 50% of issued ordinary shares
  • Hasna: 50% of issued ordinary shares

The Founders have agreed an equal 50/50 split, recognising that (a) Milan built the working prototype contributing significant prior IP, (b) Hasna brings the regulatory-domain expertise and is the future full-time operator, and (c) the two contributions are commercially equivalent across the lifetime of the Business notwithstanding short-term time commitment differences.

Governance note: A 50/50 split requires robust deadlock mechanics (see Clause 11.3) because no Founder can outvote the other. The Reserved Matters framework in Schedule 1 is designed for this — most decisions either fall within one Founder's domain authority (Clause 4.2) or require mutual agreement (Clause 4.3 / Schedule 1). The shotgun clause in 11.3 is the ultimate backstop.

2.2 Capital subscription

Each Founder shall subscribe at par value (£0.0001 per share or such other nominal value as is set on Incorporation) for their respective Founders' Shares. Total founder capital subscription at Incorporation is £8,000, split £4,000 each pro rata to their equity percentages, to be paid in full at Incorporation.

2.3 SEIS / EIS

The Founders intend for the Company to seek SEIS advance assurance at Incorporation, and for the £8,000 capital subscription to qualify for SEIS relief where eligible. Each Founder will claim their proportionate SEIS tax relief on their personal tax return for the relevant year.

3. Vesting

3.1 Reverse vesting

Notwithstanding that the Founders' Shares are issued in full at Incorporation, each Founder's shares are subject to reverse vesting over the Vesting Period. Unvested shares are subject to repurchase by the Company at nominal value if the Founder ceases to be active in the Business as a Bad Leaver.

3.2 Vesting schedule

  • Cliff (months 1–12 from the Effective Date): Zero shares are vested. If a Founder leaves as a Bad Leaver during the Cliff, the Company may repurchase 100% of that Founder's shares at par value.
  • Monthly thereafter (months 13–48): Shares vest in equal monthly instalments. At the end of month 48, all shares are fully vested.

3.3 Acceleration

100% of unvested shares accelerate to vested on:

  • The Company's acquisition by a third party (single-trigger acceleration), or
  • A material change in the Founder's role following a change of control, including but not limited to constructive dismissal (double-trigger acceleration).

3.4 Effective Date for vesting purposes

The Vesting Period and Cliff are calculated from the Effective Date of this Agreement (i.e. from signing), not from Incorporation. This recognises that founder labour invested before formal incorporation also vests toward the same schedule.

4. Roles & Responsibilities

4.1 Initial roles

  • Milan: Chief Technology Officer ("CTO"). Detailed expectations in Amaea CTO Working Agreement.
  • Hasna: Chief Executive Officer ("CEO"). Detailed expectations in Amaea CEO Working Agreement.

4.2 Domain authority

Each Founder has decision authority within their respective domain, subject always to Reserved Matters in Schedule 1:

Milan's domain (CTO): Technical architecture, AI systems, security, engineering hiring (within agreed budget), code quality standards, technical roadmap, technical-feasibility veto.

Hasna's domain (CEO): Commercial strategy, pricing decisions (within tier framework agreed in the financial model), customer success, marketing and content, sales and partnerships, regulatory positioning, board-facing communications.

4.3 Joint decision domains

The following sit outside either individual domain and require mutual agreement:

  • Total headcount and total people budget
  • Setting or changing the equity split
  • Issuing equity to new hires, options pools, advisors
  • Taking on debt or external financing
  • Material changes to the product strategy (e.g. pivoting away from UK IFA)
  • Acquisitions, partnerships involving equity, or any change of control
  • Replacing this Agreement, the Articles, or other constitutional documents

4.4 Peer governance, not principal/agent

For the avoidance of doubt: the Founders are peers, not in an employer/employee or principal/agent relationship with each other. Neither Founder reports to the other. Both Founders report jointly to the Board (which is composed of the Founders until external investors are admitted).

5. Time Commitment

5.1 Minimum commitment

  • Milan: A minimum of 10 hours per week pre-Incorporation; reviewed at Incorporation; expected to increase if and when the Business can support full-time founder compensation. See Amaea CTO Working Agreement for detail.
  • Hasna: Increasing commitment from current part-time through to full-time on Lucent exit (targeted Q3 2028). See Amaea CEO Working Agreement for detail.

5.2 No moonlighting in conflicting roles

Neither Founder shall engage in any other paid work, consulting, advisory role, or directorship that materially competes with, or impairs their ability to perform, their role in the Business, without the other Founder's prior written consent. Outside employment in a non-competing field (e.g. Milan's existing software engineering role) is expressly permitted.

6. Intellectual Property

6.1 Pre-Incorporation IP

Each Founder hereby:

  • Confirms that all intellectual property created in connection with the Business between the Effective Date and the Incorporation Date is held by that Founder on trust for the Company; and
  • Agrees to execute a formal IP Assignment Deed in favour of the Company on the Incorporation Date, assigning to the Company all such intellectual property absolutely and free of any encumbrance.

This includes (without limitation): source code in the Amaea app, marketing site, brand assets, documentation, the FCA knowledge base, schema designs, AI prompts, financial models, and any derivative works.

6.2 Post-Incorporation IP

All intellectual property created by either Founder in the course of their work for the Business after the Incorporation Date is the property of the Company at the moment of creation.

6.3 Pre-existing IP

Each Founder shall identify in writing, before signing this Agreement, any pre-existing intellectual property they own which they are not transferring to the Company but which is used in the Business. Such pre-existing IP is licensed royalty-free, irrevocably, to the Company for use in the Business.

6.4 Personal capacity work

Work created by a Founder that is genuinely outside the scope of the Business (e.g. unrelated open-source contributions, personal projects) remains the property of that Founder.

7. Confidentiality

Each Founder shall keep confidential, both during their tenure and indefinitely thereafter, all non-public information relating to the Business, including but not limited to: customer data, pricing, financial information, technical architecture, AI models, strategy, prospect lists, internal communications, and the terms of this Agreement itself.

This obligation does not apply to information that (a) becomes public through no fault of the Founder, (b) is required to be disclosed by law or regulator, or (c) is independently developed by the Founder without reference to confidential information.

8. Non-Compete & Non-Solicit

8.1 Non-compete

During each Founder's tenure and for twelve (12) months following their cessation, that Founder shall not, directly or indirectly, anywhere in the United Kingdom:

  • Engage in any business that competes with the core UK IFA compliance offering of the Business; or
  • Hold a material equity stake (>5%) in a competing business.
Lawyer review note: UK courts apply a reasonableness test to non-compete clauses. 12 months in the UK IFA compliance space is defensible if narrowly drawn. A lawyer should validate the geographic and sector scope.

8.2 Non-solicit

During each Founder's tenure and for twenty-four (24) months following their cessation, that Founder shall not:

  • Solicit or attempt to hire any employee, contractor, or material consultant of the Business; or
  • Solicit any customer, prospect (where active engagement existed in the 12 months pre-cessation), or partnership counterparty for a competing purpose.

9. Leaver Provisions

9.1 Bad Leaver

If a Founder ceases to be active as a Bad Leaver, the Company may, within 90 days of cessation, repurchase 100% of that Founder's shares (both vested and unvested) at the lower of:

  • £1 per share, or
  • The fair market value as determined by an independent valuation.

9.2 Good Leaver

If a Founder ceases to be active as a Good Leaver:

  • Unvested shares: the Company may repurchase at par value within 90 days of cessation.
  • Vested shares: the Good Leaver retains them, subject to (a) the non-compete in Clause 8, (b) any drag-along rights in Clause 10, and (c) any pre-emption rights in the Company's Articles of Association as they evolve.

9.3 Notice period

Either Founder wishing to step away as a Good Leaver shall give the other Founder no less than eight (8) weeks' written notice, during which they will:

  • Continue to perform their role to the same standard;
  • Assist in a written handover of their responsibilities;
  • Assist in the search and onboarding of a suitable replacement if and as required.

10. Drag-Along & Tag-Along

10.1 Drag-along

If Founders together holding at least 75% of the issued ordinary shares accept a bona fide offer for 100% of the Company, they may require the remaining Founder to sell their shares on the same terms ("drag-along").

10.2 Tag-along

If a Founder receives an offer to sell more than 10% of their shares to a third party, the other Founder has a right to participate in that sale on the same terms, pro rata to their holding ("tag-along").

10.3 Pre-emption

Before any third-party sale, the selling Founder must first offer their shares to the other Founder on the same terms, with a 30-day acceptance window.

11. Reserved Matters & Governance

11.1 Reserved Matters

While the Founders are the only shareholders, all matters listed in Schedule 1 require the unanimous written consent of both Founders.

11.2 Board

Until external investors are admitted, the Board of the Company shall be composed of the two Founders, each as a director with one vote. The Board shall meet at least monthly. Quorum is both directors; chair rotates monthly.

11.3 Deadlock

If the Founders are unable to agree on a Reserved Matter or any other material decision after good-faith discussion over a 14-day period:

  1. The Founders shall attempt mediation through a mutually agreed mediator. Cost split equally.
  2. If mediation fails after 30 days, the Founders shall attempt non-binding expert determination through a sector-specialist (e.g. a fintech-experienced NED or industry expert).
  3. If determination fails, either Founder may serve a buy-sell ("shotgun") notice: the Founder serving the notice offers to buy the other Founder's shares at a price per share. The receiving Founder must, within 30 days, either (a) accept the offer and sell, or (b) buy the offering Founder's shares at the same price.
Drafting note: Shotgun clauses are nuclear options and rarely invoked. Their presence usually motivates resolution through earlier steps. A lawyer may suggest alternative deadlock mechanics; this is a robust default.

12. Personal Relationship

The Founders acknowledge that they share a personal relationship in addition to their professional one. Both Founders agree to:

  • Separate company governance from personal dynamics. Decisions about the Business are made on the merits, not influenced by personal events.
  • Address tensions early. If a disagreement in one sphere is bleeding into the other, raise it within 14 days rather than allowing it to compound.
  • Protect the personal relationship. The strength of the personal relationship outside the Business is a non-negotiable priority for both Founders.

If the personal relationship materially changes (e.g. ends), the Founders agree that this does not in itself trigger any leaver provisions or change ownership. Both Founders retain their roles and equity subject only to the standard Bad Leaver / Good Leaver / non-compete provisions of this Agreement.

The Founders further agree to revisit this clause within 30 days of any material change in the personal relationship to confirm — in writing — that the Business arrangements continue unchanged, or to negotiate adjustments in good faith.

13. Future Founders & Investors

13.1 New equity issuance

Any new shares issued (to new hires, advisors, an options pool, or external investors) shall dilute both Founders pro rata to their then-current holdings, unless both Founders agree otherwise in writing.

13.2 Options pool

The Founders agree in principle to create an EMI-eligible employee options pool of up to 10% of fully diluted shares prior to any external funding round, with the timing and exact size to be agreed between the Founders.

13.3 Investor admission

The admission of external investors (whether by equity, convertible note, SAFE, or otherwise) is a Reserved Matter (Schedule 1) and requires mutual agreement. The Founders confirm their current stance is bootstrap-first — see Funding stance memo / intranet.

14. Term & Termination

This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and continues in force until:

  • Both Founders agree in writing to terminate;
  • A change of control completes (in which case the Agreement is replaced by the new shareholder framework);
  • The Business is wound up or dissolved;
  • Replaced by a successor agreement signed by both Founders.

Cessation of a Founder's active role does not terminate this Agreement as to that Founder's residual shareholding, leaver, confidentiality, and non-compete obligations.

15. General Provisions

15.1 Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of England & Wales and any dispute shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the English courts (subject to the dispute resolution mechanics in Clause 11.3).

15.2 Independent advice

Each Founder confirms they have had the opportunity to take independent legal advice before signing.

15.3 Counterparts and electronic signature

This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic means. Electronic signature (DocuSign, HelloSign, or equivalent) is acceptable provided both Founders sign the same final form.

15.4 Severability

If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder continues in full force.

15.5 Entire agreement

This Agreement supersedes all prior agreements, drafts, and understandings between the Founders relating to the Business, including the earlier "Milan's Contract" working agreement. Going forward, day-to-day expectations are documented in:

  • Amaea CTO Working Agreement (Milan)
  • Amaea CEO Working Agreement (Hasna)

These working agreements are subordinate to this Agreement and must be consistent with it.

15.6 Amendment

This Agreement may only be amended by written agreement signed by both Founders.

Schedule 1 — Reserved Matters

The following decisions require unanimous written consent of both Founders while they remain the only shareholders:

  1. Issuing new shares, granting options, or creating any class of equity.
  2. Borrowing or taking on debt in excess of £25,000 in aggregate.
  3. Acquiring or disposing of assets valued at more than £25,000 (other than ordinary-course operations).
  4. Entering into any commitment exceeding £25,000 outside the agreed budget.
  5. Paying dividends or making distributions.
  6. Changing the registered office, principal place of business, or governing law.
  7. Adopting or materially changing the Articles of Association.
  8. Filing for insolvency, administration, or winding up.
  9. Hiring or terminating any employee earning over £75,000 base salary (or equivalent).
  10. Entering into any partnership, joint venture, or licensing arrangement involving equity.
  11. Setting Founder compensation or dividends to Founders.
  12. Approving or amending the annual budget.
  13. Approving the annual financial statements.
  14. Changing the size or composition of the Board.
  15. Admitting any external investor.
  16. Any change of control or sale of the Business.
  17. Material pivot away from the UK IFA compliance market.
