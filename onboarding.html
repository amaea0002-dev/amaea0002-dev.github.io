Welcome to Amaea.

This intranet is the team's reference for everything we've decided, built, and are planning. Five documents will get you from zero to operational in an afternoon. · Last reviewed 22 May 2026.

Where you are

Amaea is a pre-launch FCA compliance platform for UK financial advisers. Public launch is September 2028. Today the founding team is four people: Hasna (CEO) and Milan (CTO) are full-time co-founders; two contractors. Investors and design partners have read-only access on shared links.
