Brand Guidelines

Who we are, what we believe, and how we present ourselves. Every element of this document is intentional.

The foundation

What we stand for

Everything we build and every decision we make is rooted in these three statements.

Mission

To remove chaos from compliance.

Our reason for existing. If a decision doesn't help remove chaos from compliance, it is not our decision to make.

Vision

To be the platform that powers compliance for FCA regulated industries.

Starting with UK IFAs. Expanding to every FCA-regulated sector that follows.

Brand tagline

Stay compliant. Stay confident.

What we believe

Values

These are the principles that shape how we work and how we treat each other, every day, not just when it is easy.

◻

Simplicity

We make complex things clear: in our product and in how we work. Clarity is our competitive advantage.

◆

Integrity

Trust is the foundation of compliance and of our relationships. We do what we say, and we say what we mean.

◎

Build for People

We build technology that genuinely helps people do their jobs better. Real problems, real solutions.

△

Respect

We treat each other with professionalism, fairness, and trust, especially when we disagree.

↗

Learn and Improve

We continuously develop ourselves, our product, and our thinking. Standing still is falling behind.

How we work

Operating Principles

These are behaviours, not aspirations.

01

Work Flexibly, Deliver Consistently

We trust people to manage their time in ways that suit them. What matters is delivering high-quality work and meeting commitments.

02

Decisions Backed by Evidence

Important decisions should be supported by data, reasoning, and evidence, not hierarchy or assumptions.

03

Sustainable Performance

Great work should not require burnout. We aim for sustainable pace and healthy workloads. We build for the long game.

04

Share Knowledge

Information should be accessible and shared so everyone can learn, contribute, and make better decisions.

05

Develop People

We invest in growth through feedback, learning, and clear career development. We want people to be better for having been here.

06

Solve Real Problems

Our focus is always on solving meaningful problems for the people who rely on our product, not what is easy to demo.

The lines

Non-Negotiables

These are the conditions under which Amaea operates. All of them. Always.

No.

Politics

Decisions are made based on evidence and what is best for the company and our users, not ego, hierarchy, or internal politics.

No.

Disrespect

Professionalism and fairness are expected at all times. We treat each other with respect, especially when we disagree.

No.

Avoiding Responsibility

If something goes wrong, we address it and fix it. Blame and defensiveness are not productive. Ownership is.

No.

Burnout Culture

Sustainable performance matters. Overtime should not be the default. We protect each other's capacity to perform over time.

No.

Silos

Knowledge should be shared. Collaboration is how we build better systems. Hoarding information helps no one.
