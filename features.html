26 features · 4 priority groups · Sept 2028 launch

Amaea Feature Map

Every named feature across the product, grouped by where it sits in the sales funnel: what closes deals, what wins them, what retains firms, what's future. These names are internal; they help us plan, pitch, and stay aligned. Firms just see the product. · Last reviewed 18 May 2026.

Why features are prioritised this way

Every feature on this page ladders to one of four strategic outcomes. We prioritise top-down — outcome 1 has to be airtight before outcome 2 matters.

1 · Make Amaea usable on day one

Dashboard, client journey, doc import + extraction, flag system, AI assistant. Live in the MVP today. Without these the product isn't real.

2 · Make Amaea valuable enough to switch from spreadsheets

Annual review auto-generation, Consumer Duty annual assessment, second integration. Phase 2 — Q3–Q4 2026. Builds switching cost.

3 · Make Amaea scale beyond 15-adviser firms

Board packs, FCA visit toolkit, custom reports, full integration suite, multi-adviser management. Phase 3 — 2027. Opens the Scale tier and the next segment up.

4 · Earn the premium tier at launch

Enterprise tier, custom AI training, FCA mock audit, dedicated Customer Success. Phase 4 — Q1–Q3 2028. The first three phases earn the right to charge a premium.

Cut rule: if a feature doesn't ladder to a specific outcome above, it doesn't get built. We've already cut several "nice to have" ideas — feature scope discipline is what makes the Sept 2028 launch date realistic.

What closes the first deal · Day 1 essentials
Pulse #
Live compliance health score
Live

The headline number. A single score out of 100 that tells a compliance officer exactly how their firm is doing at a glance — broken down across annual reviews, documentation, suitability, and regulatory deadlines. The first thing they see when they log in.

Operations Priority 1
Counsel #
FCA-grounded AI compliance assistant
Live

The AI that answers any compliance question — grounded in actual FCA rules, not general knowledge. Every answer cites the specific rule it draws from. Compliance officers ask it things they'd otherwise pay a solicitor £500/hour for. The "wow" moment in every demo.

Intelligence Priority 2
Sentinel #
Automated risk flag engine
Live

Monitors the client book continuously and surfaces compliance breaches automatically — overdue reviews, missing documents, Consumer Duty gaps, vulnerable clients without assessments. Turns compliance from reactive firefighting into proactive monitoring.

Intelligence Priority 3
Client #
Full client compliance profiles
Live

Every client in one place — their risk profile, vulnerability status, review history, missing documents, and compliance flags. Searchable, filterable by status, vulnerability, and service type. The operational heart of the product that advisers use daily.

Operations Priority 4
Guard #
Vulnerable client and Consumer Duty tracking
Live

Tracks every client flagged as vulnerable, their vulnerability driver (health, financial, life event, capability, resilience), and whether their Consumer Duty assessment is complete and current. Directly addresses the FCA's biggest current enforcement focus area.

Operations Priority 5
Review #
Annual review management
Live

Tracks every client's annual review status — when it's due, whether it's overdue, how long it's been outstanding. The single biggest compliance headache for IFA firms under COBS 9.5. Sentinel flags breaches automatically; Review gives advisers the full picture to act on them.

Operations Priority 6
Scribe #
AI-drafted compliance reports
Live

Generates the reports compliance officers actually need to produce. Full RMAR pre-population today: Sections B (P&L), D (Regulatory capital), E (PII), G (Training & Competence), and H (COBS / complaints) — auto-filled from extracted documents and firm-entered figures, with combined draft view and PDF export. Plus AI-drafted Consumer Duty board reports, suitability review summaries, vulnerable-client reports, and a compliance activity log. Each report has Concise / Detailed toggle and an embedded RAG-grounded analysis section.

Reporting Priority 7
Trail #
Immutable FCA-grade audit trail
Live

Every compliance event is logged permanently and cannot be altered. When the FCA asks "what did you know and when?", Trail is the answer. Built on an append-only database architecture. Not glamorous — but the feature that enterprise compliance officers and their PI insurers care most about.

Data Priority 8
What wins deals · The differentiators
Horizon #
Regulatory change detection and impact analysis
Building

Monitors FCA publications hourly. When new guidance drops, Horizon analyses what it means for that specific firm — which clients are affected, which gaps it creates, what needs to happen by when — and sends a firm-specific alert with a ready-made action plan. What currently costs firms £8-25k in legal fees per regulatory update, included in the subscription. The single most important sales-closing feature.

Intelligence Top differentiator
Embed #
RAG knowledge base — the AI foundation
Live · deepening

The vector database that powers Counsel and Horizon. Contains the FCA Handbook (14 sourcebooks), all Consumer Duty publications, enforcement notices, Dear CEO letters, finalised guidance, thematic reviews, and firm-specific data — all embedded via Voyage AI (voyage-3-lite, 512-dim) and searchable. What separates Amaea from a ChatGPT wrapper: every AI response is grounded in documents, not memory. Live in production at 11,645 chunks across 150 sources (23% of the 50,000 launch target). Publications + enforcement context are well covered; deeper FCA Handbook ingestion is the next ingestion pass.

Data AI foundation
Sync #
Intelliflo and practice system integrations
Planned

Connects Amaea to the systems IFA firms already use — Intelliflo, SharePoint, Salesforce, Assureweb. Client data flows in automatically; compliance monitoring starts from day one without manual data entry. The Intelliflo integration is the most important and most requested. Firms will not switch from Intelliflo; they need Amaea to work alongside it.

Data Enterprise essential
Dispatch #
Automated alerts and compliance notifications
Planned

Sends the right alert to the right person at the right time — review deadlines approaching, new risk flags raised, regulatory changes impacting the firm, weekly compliance digest email. The weekly digest lands every Monday morning and keeps compliance officers accountable to their Amaea dashboard even when they haven't logged in.

Reporting
Vault #
Document storage and expiry tracking
Live

Stores every compliance document against a client record — suitability letters, fact finds, client agreements, Consumer Duty assessments — and tracks when they expire. Sentinel flags expired documents automatically. Storage limits vary by tier: 5GB Essential, 50GB Professional, 200GB Scale.

Operations
Signal #
Compliance analytics and performance trends
Planned

The management view — trends in the firm's compliance health score over time, adviser performance breakdowns, review completion rates, flag resolution times. Both descriptive (what happened) and predictive (what is likely to happen in the next 60 days based on current trajectory). Principals and compliance officers use this for board-level reporting.

What retains firms and enables scale
Shield #
Multi-tenant security and data isolation
Live

Every firm's data is completely isolated from every other firm's. Row-Level Security in the database means Firm A can never see Firm B's clients — even if there is a bug in the application layer. For regulated firms, this is not a nice-to-have; it's a legal requirement. Enterprise buyers will send a security questionnaire before signing.

Platform
Access #
Role-based access control
Live

Different users see different things based on their role. Compliance Officers can edit reports and override flags. Advisers see their own clients only. Principals see the full firm view. A partner reviewing Amaea's data cannot accidentally access client data from another adviser's book. Firms with multiple advisers will not purchase without this.

Platform
Portal #
Firm administration, settings, and billing
Live

The settings and admin layer — adding users, configuring notification thresholds (what triggers a risk flag, how soon overdue alerts fire), managing the subscription tier, and viewing billing history. Firms self-serve their own setup without needing to contact Amaea support for every change.

Platform
Desk #
Compliance officer task management
Planned · Scale tier

Turns flags and alerts into assigned, tracked tasks. A compliance officer sees what needs doing, assigns it to the relevant adviser, sets a deadline, and tracks completion. When an FCA auditor asks "how do you ensure flagged issues are resolved?", Desk is the audit trail for remediation workflow — not just identification.

Operations
Connect #
FCA Register verification and onboarding
Planned

Verifies every new firm's FCA registration number against the Financial Services Register during onboarding. Ensures Amaea is only used by authorised firms, and pre-populates the firm profile with their registered details, permitted activities, and regulatory status. Reduces onboarding friction and prevents misuse.

Future / post-launch — Year 2 and beyond
Foresight #
Predictive compliance risk
Year 2

Predicts which clients are likely to become compliance problems before they do — based on historical patterns, review trajectories, and adviser behaviour. Rather than flagging a breach that has already happened, Foresight warns 30-60 days in advance. Requires sufficient historical data to train the model — approximately 12 months of real firm data.

Intelligence
Verify #
Cryptographically verifiable compliance attestation
Year 2+

A cryptographically signed compliance signal that a firm can share with consumers — a live, verifiable attestation that Amaea has continuously monitored their compliance position. By 2037, being Amaea-verified is expected to become a trust signal consumers actively look for when choosing an IFA. The feature that makes Amaea infrastructure, not just software.

Intelligence
Lens #
Cross-firm benchmarking and systemic intelligence
Year 2+ · Enterprise

Anonymised, aggregated intelligence across the Amaea firm base. "Your annual review completion rate is in the bottom 30% of similar-sized IFA firms." "Firms with your adviser-to-client ratio have a 3.4x higher Consumer Duty breach rate." Requires scale — only meaningful once several hundred firms are on the platform.

Intelligence
Scale #
Enterprise infrastructure and SLAs
Enterprise tier

Dedicated infrastructure for enterprise clients — separate database instances, custom data retention policies, SLA-backed uptime guarantees with financial penalties for downtime, security review documentation packs for due diligence. Banks, insurance groups, and national IFA networks will require this before signing.

Platform
Model #
Proprietary compliance foundation model
Year 3+

The point at which Amaea stops being a Claude API customer and becomes a model company. A foundation model trained from scratch on Amaea's proprietary compliance dataset — every query, every flag accepted or dismissed, every report generated by real firms over 3+ years. Categorically more accurate than a general model on compliance tasks. The irreplaceable competitive moat.

Internal tool, not visible to clients.
