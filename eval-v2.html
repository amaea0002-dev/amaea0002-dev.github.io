What is the difference between 'avoiding foreseeable harm' and 'acting in the customer's best interest' under Consumer Duty?

What's the FCA's expectation on past performance in promotions?

COBS 4.6.2 R prohibits reliance on past performance as a guide to future performance unless the promotion includes a clear and prominent warning to that effect. The past performance data must not be the most prominent feature of the communication, and it must cover a sufficient period to give a balanced picture (typically at least five years, or the full life of the investment if shorter). Under PRIN 12 (Consumer Duty) and the FCA's Financial Promotions guidance, the fair, clear and not misleading standard (COBS 4.2.1 R) overrides everything — so even technically compliant past-performance disclosures can still breach PRIN 12 if the overall impression could cause a retail client to form an unrealistic expectation of future returns.

EVAL-098 Decumulation and Retirement medium

What is MPAA and when is it triggered?

The Money Purchase Annual Allowance (MPAA) is a reduced annual allowance — currently £10,000 — that applies to defined contribution pension contributions once triggered, replacing the standard £60,000 annual allowance for money purchase inputs. It does not restrict defined benefit accrual, which remains subject to the full annual allowance. It is triggered by any of the following: • Flexible drawdown / UFPLS — taking any income from a flexi-access drawdown fund or an uncrystallised funds pension lump sum • Annuity — purchasing a flexible (investment-linked or with surrender/reduction rights) annuity • Pre-April 2015 flexible drawdown — anyone already in flexible drawdown before 6 April 2015 had the MPAA applied automatically from that date • Scheme pension from a small scheme (fewer than 12 members) where the scheme reduces the pension within the first year Taking a tax-free cash lump sum alone, purchasing a lifetime annuity with no flexibility, or moving into capped drawdown without drawing income does not trigger the MPAA. The notification obligation under Finance Act 2004 s.227B requires the scheme that triggers the MPAA to issue a "flexible access" statement to the member within 31 days; the member must then notify any other pension provider receiving contributions within 91 days.
